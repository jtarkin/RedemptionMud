Death
DEATH

When your character dies on Terra, you are reincarnated back at the Altar of the Temple of Midgaard. If you die on Dominia, you are reincarnated at the Temple of Selina in Shandalar. If you joined a clan, you will be reincarnated inside your clan hall or outpost. Your corpse is left behind in the room where you were killed, together with all of your equipment.

Any spells which were affecting you are canceled by death.

You lose experience points for dying. The amount you lose is one-quarter of the way back to the beginning of your level.

Corpses decay after time, and the objects inside corpses decay with them. Player corpses last 25 to 40 real-time minutes. Half of your carried gold is left in your corpse; the other half is lost.

Only the player who died, or someone in the same group, can retrieve objects from a corpse. Clanners may loot other clanners' corpses. If the game crashed after you died, it's a good bet that you lost it. Please do not go begging to an immortal to help you get your equipment back — it is not their duty to get it back for you.

Equipment Damage
ITEM DAMAGE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DAMAGED

Many spells and element effects can damage or destroy your worn equipment. There are two outcomes: temporary damage and permanent destruction.

Temporarily damaged items are unequipped and cannot be reworn until the effect wears off (1–3 ticks). This affects clothing hit by acid-based attacks, and jewelry hit by shock/lightning-based attacks.

Destroyed items are consumed outright and cannot be recovered. This affects containers, wands, and scrolls hit by acid; potions and drinks hit by cold or earth; containers, potions, scrolls, staves, wands, food, and pills hit by fire; and wands and staves hit by shock/lightning.

Wind-based effects can fling potions and pills to an adjacent room. Items with the BURN_PROOF flag are immune to most element-based destruction.

Escaping
FLEE RESCUE
fleeAttempts to escape from combat in a random direction. Success is not guaranteed.
rescue <character>Pulls another character out of combat, redirecting the enemy's attention to you.

Once a fight starts you cannot simply walk away. Use flee to escape, or ask another player to rescue you. You can also use recall, but it is less likely to succeed and costs more experience points than fleeing.

If you lose your link during a fight, your character will keep fighting and attempt to recall from time to time — with reduced chances and much greater experience loss.

WIMPY
wimpySets your wimpy value to 20% of your maximum hit points.
wimpy <number>Sets a specific wimpy threshold. Cannot exceed 50% of your total hit points.

When your hit points drop below your wimpy value, your character will automatically attempt to flee. This only triggers when you are not in a wait state — combat commands like cast, trip, and bash, or being tripped or bashed, will suppress the auto-flee. Wimpy is not a guarantee of escape. Setting wimpy to 1hp will not save you; you will die.

See also: recall can sometimes get you out of a fight as an alternative to fleeing — however, recall will fail if you are cursed. Curse is a malediction spell; see Maledictions for the full list of debuffs that can affect your ability to escape. Spellcasters may also use blink as a quick escape option, or gate to teleport directly to another character on the continent.

KILLER THIEF

A (KILLER) flag marks someone who was aggressive toward another player. Guards and protectors will be aggressive toward flagged characters.

A (THIEF) flag marks someone who attempted to steal from another player and failed. Anyone with only a THIEF flag will receive a KILLER flag if attacked.

To remove either flag, you must die to another player other than a fellow clan mate. In unusual circumstances an immortal may pardon the flag, provided there is a compelling reason.

Each weapon skill applies to a specific group of armaments and determines how well a character fights with that weapon. The weaponsmaster group provides talent in all weapons (save exotics and marksmanship). If the character has the parry skill, they can parry incoming attacks.

axeThe use of axes, ranging from hand to great (but not halberds).
daggerThe use of knives, daggers, and other stabbing weapons.
flailSkill in ball-and-chain type weapons.
maceIncludes clubs and hammers as well as maces.
polearmThe use of pole weapons (except spears), including halberds.
spearCovers both spears and staves, but not polearms.
swordThe warrior's standby, from rapier to claymore.
whipThe use of whips, chains, and bullwhips.
weaponsmasterSkill group covering all weapons listed above.
exotic *Strange magical weapons. Cannot be purchased — dependent solely upon level.
marksmanship **Ranged weapons. Can only be learned by rangers.

Learned by: All

SHIELD BLOCK

Shield block is the art of parrying with a shield. Characters without this skill will not defend themselves well with a shield. All classes may learn shield block, but only warriors and clerics are particularly good at it.

Learned by: All
