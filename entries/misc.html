PKILL PKILLING PKILLER PSTEAL PSTEALING

Player killing and player stealing are allowed to all clan members (including loners), provided the victim is within 8 levels of the attacker and is also in a clan. This flags you as a KILLER or THIEF, allowing any character up to 16 levels above you to attack you, and provoking city guards and law-enforcing mobs.

Killers may only loot lootables from corpses: potions, pills, scrolls, bank notes, trash items, and anything held but not claimed by the victim. For full-loot privileges (and full-loot vulnerability), see help ruthless.

RULES

These are the rules of Redemption. For other matters, use common sense.

  1. No multiple logins, multiplaying, or helping your own characters with other characters you own — by any arrangement.
  2. Helping another player multiplay is illegal. This includes passing equipment between two characters owned by the same player.
  3. Pkill scouting is illegal. If you plan to pkill or psteal, wait offline for at least 3 minutes when switching characters. After logging in without waiting, you may not enter a pkill/psteal situation for 5 minutes.
  4. You may not play a character another player has created or leveled. Shared or transferred characters will be frozen.
  5. Running a script or client that keeps you connected while away from your computer is not allowed. Lagging, freezing, and denying are valid punishments for idling.
  6. Spamming over channels is illegal. Refusing to stop practicing skills or spells in a manner that spams the immortals is illegal.
  7. Harassment based on race, gender, or sexual orientation will not be tolerated. Reported OOC abuse can result in deletion and a possible site ban.
  8. Repeated vulgar language over open channels, in titles, or in notes is grounds for no_channel or freeze — do not expect a warning.
  9. You may not announce or speculate over open channels or in notes the characters of specific players.
  10. Accusing someone of cheating over open channels or in notes to 'all' is a punishable offense. Report suspected cheating to the immortals directly.
  11. It is illegal to attack and kill someone more than three times in an hour, including through charmies, groups, or by summoning them to a mob.
  12. Clanner and nonclanner interference is prohibited. This includes summoning to aggressive mobs, spelling up mobs, stealing kills, passing bank notes or large sums of money (>5k), holding stolen equipment, healing or summoning from a pfight, and picking up equipment dropped in a pkill.
  13. Cheating will not be tolerated. This includes accepting favors from gods and exploiting bugs. Report bugs — then do not use them.
  14. Communicating craft ingredients or keywords to the general public is forbidden.
  15. Your presence here is a privilege, not a right. Obey orders from gods. Deletion does not require a reason. Appeals go to the implementors.
  16. Changes to these rules are at the discretion of the immortals and will be posted on the announcement board.

See also: help punishments

LOST PASSWORD

If you have lost the password for a character you own, contact a level 59+ immortal (Starcrossed, Cauthon) and they can reset it for you. You will be required to provide as much proof as possible that you own the character — any information you can provide about it will help your case.

CHARACTER TRADE

Character trades are allowed on Redemption. Such exchanges must be between characters of equivalent level, with approximately the same sophistication of equipment and number of abilities. Whether a trade is considered fair is negotiated between the two parties and the Immortal performing the trade. All trades must be approved and performed by a level 58+ immortal.

Both affected characters will lose 10 HP/mana/movement. The characters will be renamed, and it is up to one of the new characters to post a note to everyone stating both the old and new character names.

Note: All claimed equipment will not be adjusted to reflect the new names — all claimed equipment will be lootable. This is considered a penalty for trading.

Bard / Jukebox
PLAY BARD JUKEBOX

At the bards, you can play music over the music channel. You must be in a room where there is a bard for this to work.

play listLists the songs the bard can play.
play list artistLists songs with artist names included.
play list artist <name>Lists all songs by the indicated artist.
play <song name>Makes the bard play that song in the room.
play loud <song name>For a cost, makes the bard sing over the music channel for all to hear.
play dedicate <char> <song>For a cost, dedicates a song to a playing character.
Games
GAMES GAMBLE
game slots <slot name>Plays a slot machine in the current room.
game highdice <bet>Places a bet on a high dice game against the dealer in the room.
SLOTS SLOT MACHINE

Each slot machine costs a set amount of gold to play and can be 3-bar or 5-bar. Each bar has 10 different items. Matching all bars wins the jackpot. Machines may have partial winning enabled, allowing smaller prizes for partial matches. If freeze jackpot is enabled, the jackpot remains fixed until won. Type examine <slot machine name> to see a machine's settings.

HIGHDICE HIGH DICE

A dice dealer must be present in the room. Both you and the dealer automatically roll two dice — the higher total wins. In a tie, the dealer wins. Bets range from 10 to 1,000 gold.

Punishments
NOQUIT

If a NO_QUIT flag is added to your playerfile, there is a reason — mostly because the immortal talking to you doesn't want you to quit. You may still lose link or hang up, but those aren't game commands.

Also, when pkilling, you will not be able to quit until 5 ticks later. This prevents people from fleeing and quitting to escape a pkill.
