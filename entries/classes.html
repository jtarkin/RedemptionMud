Class Change
CLASS CHANGE

Syntax: class <classname>

At a trainer, a character who has reached level 25 can change to any other class. Artificer is not available for class change.

Each class you play is tracked independently. When you switch away from a class, your current level, exp, cp, trains, practices, stats, hp, mana, move, and known skill groups are all saved for that class. When you return to a class you have played before, all of that is fully restored.

Skill percentages (how well you know each skill) are shared across all classes and are never reset by class change.

If you switch to a class you have never played before, you begin at level 1 with no saved progression.

On class change:

-You must not be in combat or recently engaged in PK
-Any gear the new class cannot wear is automatically removed to inventory
-Painted and grafted temporary items are destroyed
-All active effects on you are removed
-If you have a remort, remort skills not yet learned are set to 1%

The command requires confirmation: type it once to see a preview of your level and exp rate in the new class, then repeat to confirm.

See also: help class, help reclass

Reclass Options
RECLASS CLASS REMORT

Syntax: reclass

Once you reach level 51 you may reclass your character to gain several new abilities and also new restrictions. This is the counterpart to remorting. You may remort and reclass in any order and choose to do one, the other, or both.

To reclass will require a specific number of quest points. You can find out how many by typing reclass once you are level 51.

Like remorting, reclassing will send your character back to level one. It will remove all your equipment and put it in your inventory, and reset all skills to 1% except for your starting weapon. Your hours will be reset, your trains set to 3, and practices to 5. Any skills which you gained prior to reclassing will be added to your creation points, possibly raising your xp/level. Your stats (strength, etc) will also be reset to the default for your race. Any trained cp will not be taken into consideration for your new xp/level.

Monk
MONK DEFAULT MONK BASIC

The monk is both a new and old discipline in this world. With the discovery of more and more ancient relics and crafted items being unleashed, those who have and those who have not have grown apart in stature and strength. Enter the monk. Using ancient disciplines developed long ago, the monk is able to use his or her own body in ways that rival most modern weaponry. Monks are specialists at fighting against the martial classes, but have no special affinity for magic, making it an ideal tool to use against them.

The biggest strength of the monk lies in the flexibility of their fighting styles. With great ease they can switch roles from defensive turtles to high powered damage dealers and anywhere in between. They require no weapons and function best without any, although they retain proficiency with exotic weapons of all kinds.

Hand to Hand — The monk's primary weapon. Deals more damage unarmed than any other class; can fully parry and disarm at no penalty
Boost — Battle cry that increases hit/dam for one round
Kick — A swift kick to deal additional damage between rounds
Trip — Send your opponent to the floor
Hide — Remain out of sight while stationary
Dodge — Nimbly avoiding attacks
Enhanced Damage — Increases the base damage of each attack
Intense Damage — A further damage bonus on top of enhanced damage
Stances — A variety of fighting styles for all situations (Turtle, Tiger, Mantis)
Chi Blasts — Different ways to channel chi to enhance fighting (Chi: Ei, Chi: Kaze)
Boost — Strike with a loud kiai to boost your next round of attacks

SAMURAI MONK

Without a feudal system to restrict their actions, modern samurai still do their best to follow a strict code of honor. Samurai start out weak, but as they advance, they unlock the ability to use katanas and sheathes. These blades are legendary in power but take great effort to maintain and upgrade. Samurai must continuously craft and reforge their katanas throughout their advancement in order to succeed.

The samurai fighting style is unique, relying on very few blows to slay a foe, and instead focusing on intensity and accuracy to make the most of the hair-splitting katana.

Stance: Kensai — A stance that strongly boosts accuracy
Katana — The weapon skill to use katanas; may be specialized
Doublestrike — A successful katana strike deals damage twice
Extreme Damage — Increases the base damage done
Third Eye — Transfer your blindness to your foe
Quicken — Speed yourself up while slowing your foe
Seppuku — Ritual suicide; greatly raises hit/dam for a short time until the ritual is complete, at the cost of hp

NINJA MONK

Working in the shadows, ninjas are a mysterious class with a very powerful mixture of tanking and support abilities. Renowned for their talents in assassination and espionage, monks who take up the life of a ninja are forever marked as outlaws by civilized towns and attacked on sight by locals. Ninjas have developed strong talents with exotic weapons. Their brand of "magic" — ninjutsu — attacks foes and renders them susceptible to further damage. They can also restore allies' mana and tank physical attacks better than any other class through evasion and other skills.

Stance: Shadow — Become undetectable during combat, but more vulnerable to magic
Dual Wield — Can use two weapons (usually exotics)
Evasion — Defends against the next attack(s) that would have hit you
Blindfighting — Suffers no skill penalties while blind
Ninjutsu — Uses up a charm to inflict damage and add a random effect to your target
Instant Stand — Recover if you are bashed to the ground
Shadow Walk — Less movement used when walking between rooms

UNREMORT UNRECLASS

It is said that somewhere deep at the bottom of an abyss lies a special place where you can forsake your current remort or reclass, should you be willing to give up a part of your soul to do so.

Those who wish to do this must first find the location where it must be done. From there, the location itself will tell you how to find the item you may need to prove you are willing to give up a portion of your soul.
