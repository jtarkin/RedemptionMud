General
CLAN CLANS

A clan is a collection of players united to follow and further a common cause. Each clan has rules and regulations enforced by the leader. If an immortal must interfere, that immortal must be level 58+ or hold a 58+ permission.

To find out more about a specific clan, type help <clan name>. There are two main clans: WARDENS and REMNANTS. More clans may exist if a mortal or immortal proposes a reasonable idea and purpose.

Joining a clan is a dangerous step. Many players (pkillers) exist to kill and harass clanners. While in the PK system you may kill anyone except non-clanned characters. Switching clans is allowed, but you must have the leader's or a recruiter's permission to join. In order for a clan mutiny to occur, a level 58+ immortal must be contacted for it to be considered legal and official.

See also: help loner, help mutiny, help retire

MUTINY

In order for a clan mutiny to occur, a level 58+ immortal must be contacted. The mutiny follows these guidelines:

1. The challenger cannot be more than 10 levels below the clan leader, and must supply the immortals with the names of at least one level 51+ remort/reclass clan member who supports the mutiny. The challenger must not hold a leadership or recruiter position in another clan at the time of the note.

2. The mutiny is not official until the immortals receive written confirmation from supporters and a note has been posted to the entire clan announcing the attempt.

3. The current leader must accept the challenge. If they refuse, leadership passes to the challenger and the leader is removed from the clan. If no response is received within 2 weeks, the challenger is automatically declared the winner.

4. The mutiny is held at the earliest date agreeable to both parties and a level 58+ immortal. Fights will not be postponed to allow leveling. If the fight does not occur within 2 weeks of the immortal announcement, the delaying party is declared the loser.

5. Once a mutiny is declared, all recruiting halts. The challenger and supporters cannot be removed from the clan, killed, looted, or stolen from by clanmates. Violators will be lonered.

6. If the challenger or their supporters attack a clanmate outside of agreed duels or immortal-run tourneys, they will be lonered and the mutiny called off.

7. If the current leader accepts: if the challenger loses they must delete; if the challenger wins, the leader is lonered and the challenger becomes leader.

8. The leader may appoint a champion to fight in their place, provided the champion agrees and was a clan member when the mutiny was declared.

9. The duel is one-on-one with no wands, staffs, scrolls, potions, charged craft items, throwing objects, or pills (except for spelling up before the fight). Ammunition for ranged attacks is allowed but may not be refilled. The only charmies allowed are troll arms/legs. The fight is held in a safe room and no looting is allowed.

10. No outside interference is permitted during the mutiny fight. Anyone engaged in interference will be frozen or similarly punished.

11. Both fighters will be cancelled prior to the fight, then restored and allowed to spell up. Worship spells are not permitted — anyone caught with one active will be disqualified.

12. The fight must begin within 10 seconds of the immortal saying go. Lag is the only exception. The fight may be briefly paused by skills but must resume within 2 ticks.

13. Anything not explicitly disallowed is allowed. Questionable situations should be raised with the immortal running the mutiny.

14. After a successful mutiny, the new leader has 24 hours to make clan membership changes. No new mutiny may be called during this period.

15. Before declaring a mutiny, the mutineer must loner all their alts from other clans.

16. If the loser is the one who declared the mutiny, that character must delete. Failure to do so results in permanent freeze.

See also: help clan

Clan Lore
REMNANT REMNANTS

When the dragonwar ended and most of Terra was destroyed, the remnants of Bosco's army still survived. Traveling together, they eventually made camp on the western side of Terra and established a military base. That army is now known as the Remnants.

History

The cleric Xerges wandered the land of Terra after the war, gradually gathering more of Bosco's children until they settled and began rebuilding. As time passed, Xerges grew tired and fatigued from years of battle and passed leadership to a druid named Greyson. Under Greyson the clan grew further, but much like his predecessor he too had seen too many battles. He became reclusive, and at times the clan felt as though it had no leader.

From that absence, another cleric stepped forward and took the reins. Greyson stepped down to an advisor role. For a time, it seemed almost peaceful — until rumblings of Selina's children banding together in the west heralded the rise of a rival faction. Warden, they called themselves.

Rules

  1. Attacking unfinished characters is not allowed. Unfinished characters on either continent are to be left alone.
  2. Wardens must be expelled from Terra. They may pass through our continent only if assisting us or with explicit permission.
  3. Assist all newbies and loners to the best of your ability.
  4. Respect your clanmates.
  5. Maximum of 2 charmies when attacking other players, except when defending a hall break in progress.
  6. Members are responsible for any player corpses they create. Corpses must be returned to the player in person.
  7. Looting of equipment is not allowed. Only the following may be taken: wands, staves, scrolls, throwing items, gems, gold, and bank notes.
  8. Equipment retained via disarm, gravitate, swap, heat metal, detonate, etc. must be returned to the owner. A fee of up to 2000 gold per item is acceptable. Equipment belonging to Wardens may be held until they return to Dominia.

Recruitment

Remnants welcome anyone who upholds the clan's honor. No fees or quests are required to join. Contact the clan leader or a recruiter to apply. A brief quiz of the clan rules may be given at time of request.

WARDEN WARDENS

The Wardens trace their origins to the followers of Selina who helped win the great dragonwar through unity and the hivemind of the slivers who ruled Dominia. When Bosco restored the continent of Terra and a new army began to grow there, the wards of Selina grew fearful of another invasion. Determined to protect Dominia from being overrun again, they banded together and took the name Wardens.

Where the Remnants are the surviving force of Bosco's fallen army, the Wardens stand as the defenders of Selina's lands. The two clans have been at odds since their founding, each claiming rightful dominion over the continent's future.
