Maledictions
HELP MALEDICTIONS, HELP CURSE, HELP DEBUFFS

Maledictions are debuff spells that impose negative effects on their target — weakening stats, preventing actions, or hampering combat ability. Common examples include curse, blind, poison, and slow.

Every malediction is also an aggressive spell — casting one on another character will immediately start combat, even if you had no hostile intent. Use them carefully in mixed company.
