Giant
HELP GIANT

Giants are one of the largest races, ranging from 8 to 11 feet in height. They are well-suited for warriors, though any class may be a giant. Giants are strong and tough but not particularly bright or nimble. Their huge frames grant them natural resistance to both cold and fire, though they are vulnerable to lightning.

Giants begin with the fast healing, bash, and bashdoor skills.

Creation cost: 6 points

Human
HELP HUMAN

Humans are the most common race and the most versatile. They have no special resistances or innate abilities, but they excel in every class and can train their primary stats higher than any other race.

Creation cost: 0 points

Elf
HELP ELF

Elves are nimble and magically attuned, with infravision from their glowing red eyes. Their magical nature grants them resistance to charm and mental effects, but their slender builds make them vulnerable to iron. Elves begin with the sword and sneak skills and excel in magic-based classes.

Creation cost: 8 points

Dwarf
HELP DWARF

Dwarves are short, stocky demi-humans known for their foul temper and glowing red eyes from their infravision. They are highly resilient, with a natural resistance to both poison and disease. However, their dense frames make them vulnerable to drowning. Dwarves have high strength and constitution. They start with the berserk and axe skills.

Creation cost: 8 points

Troll
HELP TROLL

Trolls are a medium-sized, stocky race descended from dwarves corrupted by chaos magic ages ago. They possess surprising strength but lack patience and strategy. Their infravision allows them to see in the dark. Trolls have an improved immune system granting disease resistance and fast regeneration. As a trade-off, fire disrupts their healing abilities. All trolls favor the axe as their totem weapon and begin with the regeneration and axe skills.

Creation cost: 6 points

Gargoyle
HELP GARGOYLE

Gargoyles are large, xenophobic beings native to the mountains of Dominia. They are powerful fighters who take to the air and dive upon their prey, favoring polearm weapons that mirror their fearsome limbs. Gargoyles have infravision. All gargoyles can invoke the earth to become living stone, boosting their durability in combat. However, their magical nature leaves them vulnerable to both poison and disease. Gargoyles begin with the living stone, polearm, and enhanced damage skills.

Creation cost: 12 points

Kirre

Hidden deep in the forests of Dominia, a race of intelligent felines has flourished in total seclusion for centuries. These creatures, who identify themselves simply as "Kirre", resemble none of the known races of Dominia. Their history and origins remain shrouded in mystery and legend.

Kirre are six-legged felines, roughly 6–8 feet in length, ranging in colour from white to sable. Long tufted ears, six legs with razor sharp claws, and a prehensile tail give them a fearsome appearance. They strike quickly and without mercy. Kirre have infravision and a natural resistance to lightning, though they are vulnerable to cold.

Over time the Kirre have organized into small tribes, led by the strongest male among many females. Every seven moons their tribal leaders gather in secret. Recent discussions have centered on the "Outsiders" — other Dominian races encroaching on their ancestral hunting grounds. Rather than reveal their intelligence, the Kirre observed and planned from behind a bestial guise. Some have since chosen to reveal their true nature; a bold few have even called outsiders "friend."

Although many warriors exist among them, Kirre are also adept in subtle magic, and many show a natural aptitude for rune magic. All Kirre prefer whip-like weapons for their similarity to their own tails, and begin with the whip, disarm, and hand to hand skills.

Creation point cost: 12

RACE REMORTS REMORTING

Once you reach level 51 you can remort your character and pick a new sub-race, which provides new skills and spells along with new limitations. You must have a certain number of quest points — type remort to see the requirement.

Remorting removes all worn equipment to your inventory, sets you back to level 1 (20hp 100mana 50mv), resets all skills/spells to 1% (weapon resets to 40%), and gives you the main weapon for your class. Hours reset to 0, wimpy to 0, and you receive 3 trains and 5 practices. You keep your clan affiliation and can be pkilled at level 1. Killer/thief flags are removed. Any skills or spells gained after creation are added to your remort CP.

Aasimar are the descendants of celestial beings — mortals touched by divine blood in ages past. Though most live unremarkable lives, some are called to a greater destiny, manifesting the power of their heritage through remort.

SizeMedium
IntrinsicsDark vision, Flight
ResistancesLight damage
VulnerabilitiesEnergy damage
Starting SkillsSword, Flurry, Guide

Creation cost: 6 points

Aasimar begin their lives as capable fighters with an innate connection to the divine. Their true potential is unlocked at remort, when they choose to embrace either their angelic heritage or fall from grace.

RISEN AASIMAR REMORT

Risen have embraced their celestial heritage, ascending toward their divine origin. They gain a 25% bonus to divine and holy damage spells, and take 33% reduced damage from divine damage.

Marid
MARID

From deep beneath the Dragon Sea, the Marid have emerged with stories told of horrific destruction — their precious coral cities destroyed by an unknown evil. Unseen on the surface of Terra and Dominia for hundreds of years, the Marid are attempting to salvage what is left of their civilization and find a role in the societies of the land dwellers. As scattered refugees they are still few in number, but more emerge from the sea every week, drawing stares wherever they go.

Marid could almost pass themselves off as human, but for their odd coloration. Their features include pale white, fish-colored skin and hair ranging from deep sea blue to seaweed green. Their eyes are double-lidded but otherwise humanoid. Their ears are long and pointed — similar to elves but even larger, to help detect changes in water pressure. Marid are generally thin and rarely exceed 5 feet in height. They are considered small size, and despite their lives beneath the waves they have no fins, tail, or gills.

Marid are highly magical creatures attuned to the elements that form the world, and make powerful magic users. Despite their frail appearance their nature makes them largely resistant to damage and they tend to have high hit points for their class. All Marid are especially skilled in elemental magic and begin with the essence skill, which causes their elemental spells to have additional effects. Their command of the sea also gives all Marid the ability to cast the hurricane spell, and they begin the game knowing how to use flails.

As creatures of water, Marid enjoy a resistance to both fire damage and drowning, however they can be easily stricken down by electricity. They can travel over both land and water with ease and are the only race that can breathe both water and air.

Creation cost: 14 points  |  Remort options: Nereid, Befouler
