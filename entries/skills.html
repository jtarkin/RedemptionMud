General Skills

General skills cover a range of non-combat abilities — movement, utility, and passive techniques that don't fit neatly into combat or passive categories.

SHADOW
shadowAllows you to move silently through shadows, reducing the chance of detection while moving through rooms.

Learned by: Ranger (10), Thief (7)

ENVENOM
envenom <food or drink>Coats food or drink with poison. The consumer will suffer a mild poison effect.
envenom <weapon>Coats a weapon with venom, dealing ongoing damage as it burns through the target's bloodstream. Blade venom evaporates quickly and loses potency with repeated strikes in combat.

Learned by: Thief (8)

PENTAGRAM
pentagramTraces a chalk pentagram onto the ground. The pentagram protects everyone in the room from summoned demons.

Learned by: Invoker (20)

PEEK
peek <character>Look at someone's inventory without them knowing. Similar to look, but targets a character's carried items. Only rogues can peek inside a person's containers. There is a slight chance you may be caught.

Learned by: Warrior (6), Ranger (6), Thief (1), Runist (1)

AXE

Proficiency with axe-type weapons, improving your chance to hit and the damage you deal when wielding one.

Learned by: Warrior, Druid, Ranger (automatic) — Dwarf, Troll (racial) — Other classes may learn this via the Weaponsmaster group.

APPRAISE
appraise <item>Red dragons can use their eye for treasure to increase the value of items they carry. On success, the item's value increases by 50%. On failure, there is a 30% chance the item is destroyed outright. The item must be in your inventory and cannot be appraised beyond its original base value. Some items cannot be appraised. Costs 15 mana.

Learned by: Red Dragon

LAIR
lairBlue dragons are extremely self-reliant and may take up a lair during their travels. The dragon may only have one lair at a time. While within their lair, the blue dragon will be hard to find and enjoys a small increase in healing rate.

Learned by: Blue Dragon

GUARDIAN
guardian <player>Acts as a guardian angel to yourself or another character, protecting them from evil. Provides some protection against spell casters and minor protection from mundane attacks.

Learned by: Paladin

BRIBE
bribe <mob> <target>Persuades a mob to hunt a targeted character. Even on failure, the gold spent is lost.

Learned by: Thief

BUTCHER
butcher <corpse>Butchers a corpse on the ground into steaks that can be eaten. Useful when food is scarce.

Learned by: All classes

DRAG
drag corpse <direction>Drag a corpse in the given direction at twice the normal movement cost.

No, this is not a character dressing up in another gender's clothes. This skill allows a character to drag a corpse from room to room at twice the normal amount of movement.

Learned by: Warrior (7), Paladin (7), Ranger (7), Monk (7)

BASHDOOR
bashdoor <direction>Attempt to force open a door by bashing it. Success depends on strength and skill level.

Some doors refuse to open. With bashdoor, a character can attempt to force a door open by bashing it.

Learned by: All classes at level 25. Giants receive this skill at creation.

RETRIBUTION
retributionToggle retribution on or off. When active, you automatically attack any KILLERs and THIEFs detected in the same room.
Warning: Retribution may cause your character to act aggressively. Be mindful of where you have it toggled on.

Learned by: Warrior (28), Paladin (28), Ranger (28), Monk (28)

PAINT TATTOO LESSER GREATER POWER
paint lesserFills the first empty tattoo slot with a lesser tattoo — greatest number of bonuses, but each is small.
paint greaterFills the first empty tattoo slot with a greater tattoo — fewer bonuses, but each is much larger.
paint powerFills the first empty tattoo slot with a power tattoo — fewest bonuses, but may grant a unique power ability unavailable from any other source.

Tattoos are usable only by runists and occupy equipment slots (torso, head, arms, right wrist, left wrist). Bonuses are random and scale with level. Tattoos cannot be removed except by the destroy tattoo skill or waiting for them to fade naturally.

Learned by: Runist

POWER TATTOO

Power tattoos grant a unique bonus in addition to standard attribute bonuses. Possible power bonuses: chance to cast spells using movement instead of mana; animated tattoo creature (summoned via animate); increased healing rate; increased mana regeneration; regain hp per kill; regain mana per kill; bonus deity points per sacrifice; reduced physical damage taken; reduced magical damage taken; temporary knowledge of unknown spells; raised spell effectiveness. All tattoo bonuses are cumulative and stack.

Learned by: Runist

DESTROY TATTOO

Removes a tattoo from the runist's own body. Does not always succeed, and inflicts fierce burns on the runist when it does. Tattoos resist all other forms of removal, including death — this skill is the only way to replace an unwanted tattoo before it fades naturally.

Learned by: Runist

DESTROY MAGIC DESTROY
destroy <item>Destroys a magic item in your inventory, releasing its enchanted energy. The item must be no higher level than your own. Crafted items and items marked no-sacrifice cannot be destroyed this way.

Learned by: Ranger (5)

DISASSEMBLE
disassemble <item>Breaks down a crafted item that is claimed to you, recovering some of the materials used to make it. The item must be crafted and currently claimed to you. Costs 50 move.

Learned by: Ranger (5)

DISENCHANT
disenchant <item>Strips the magical properties from a weapon or piece of equipment, reverting it to a mundane state.

Learned by: Ranger (5)

HIDE SNEAK

Hide and sneak are related skills for remaining undetected. Hide is used while stationary and has a high success rate. Sneak is used while moving, allowing a character to pass through rooms undetected.

Learned by: Thief (5), Ranger (10), Monk (25) — Most other classes (30)

LORE

Lore is a general skill consisting of knowledge of myths and legends. Using it gives a chance to obtain information about an object — its power, uses, and value. All classes may learn lore.

Learned by: All classes

HAGGLE HAGGLING

Haggling allows a character to negotiate with merchants for better prices on purchases and sales. Thieves excel at it, though all classes may learn the skill.

Learned by: All classes

PICK LOCK

Lock picking allows a character to open secured doors without a key. Success depends on intelligence and the difficulty of the lock.

Learned by: Thief (7), Ranger (25)

LIVING STONE
stoneHardens the gargoyle's flesh to stone, greatly increasing damage resistance and improving attacks. While in stone form the gargoyle gains the properties of stone.

Learned by: Gargoyle (racial)

MARID ESSENCE ESSENCE

Marid are an elemental species with a powerful connection to the elements that make up the world. By focusing their essence, Marid invoke elemental magical effects with additional benefits. For example, any fire spells cast by a Marid can cause fire blindness and destroy potions.

Learned by: Marid (racial — all Marid begin with this skill)

CHEAT
cheatEnhances sleight-of-hand for 5 ticks, improving success at deceptive interactions such as trading and dealing.

Learned by: Thief (25)

CURE PLAGUE
cure plagueAttempt to cure the plague affliction on a target. Success chance scales with skill level.

Learned by: Paladin (2)

AIMING
aimFocus concentration to sharpen ranged accuracy at the cost of raw hitting power.

Rangers can focus their concentration to sharpen their accuracy at the cost of raw hitting power. On success, hitroll improves by roughly level/4 while damroll decreases by half that amount. The effect lasts approximately level/6 ticks. Costs 50 mana and cuts current movement in half. Elves receive a bonus to the activation chance.

Learned by: Ranger

BERSERK
berserkFly into a frenzied rage for hitroll and damroll bonuses at the cost of reduced armor class.

The character flies into a frenzied rage, gaining hitroll and damroll bonuses (level/5 each) at the cost of reduced armor class. The rage also heals a small amount of HP on activation. Duration is approximately level/6 ticks. Costs 50 mana and 50 movement. Cannot be used while calmed, while under guardian's protection, or while taunted. Dwarves receive a significant bonus to the activation chance.

Learned by: Warrior (18), Ranger (25), all other classes (30). Dwarves receive this skill at creation.

SHOOT
shoot <target>Fire a single shot from a ranged weapon at the target.

This is the command used to fire ranged weapons. See Marksmanship for more information on ranged combat.

Learned by: Ranger

Combat Skills
DRAW SHEATH
drawPulls the weapon out of your worn sheath and wields it, costing a small amount of mana depending on the sheath type.
sheathPlaces your wielded weapon into a worn sheath item.
sheath <weapon>Places a named weapon from your inventory into your worn sheath.
FOURTH ATTACK

Grants the possibility of a fourth attack per combat round. A perfect Fourth Attack does not guarantee four attacks — only very specialized warriors have access to this skill.

HUNT
hunt <name>When successful, indicates the general direction in which a target creature or character lies from your current location.

Learned by: Warrior (9), Paladin (9), Ranger (9), Thief (9), Runist (9), Monk (9)

THROW
throw <target>A remote attack initiated from a room adjacent to the target. You must be holding a throwable object first.

Learned by: All reclasses (automatic at reclass)

DIRT KICKING DIRT

Considered by some to be a cowardly skill, dirt kicking gives the clever combatant a chance to blind his opponent by casting dirt into his eyes. The blindness does not last long, but can provide an edge in combat. Dexterity helps in hitting or avoiding a dirt kick.

Learned by: Warrior (3), Ranger (3), Thief (3), Runist (5)

DISARM

Disarm is a somewhat showy and unreliable skill, designed to relieve your opponent of his weapon. The best possible chance of disarming occurs when you are skilled at both your own and your opponent's weapon.

Learned by: All classes (level 11)

DEATH BLOW
deathblow <target>A precision strike aimed at a critical point that deals significantly increased damage if it lands. Requires careful study of the target before engaging; entering combat cancels the preparation.

Learned by: Thief (35) — Assassin (automatic at reclass)

KICK

Kicking allows the adventurer to receive an extra attack in combat. A failed kick may throw an unwary fighter off-balance.

Learned by: Monk (7), Warrior (8), Paladin (8), Ranger (9), Cleric (12), Druid (12), Thief (14), Runist (10)

TRIP

Trip is a dastardly attack that uses various methods to bring an opponent down to the ground. Tripping large monsters is generally not wise, and agile targets can dodge it easily.

Learned by: Thief (5), Runist (7), Warrior (15), Paladin (15), Ranger (15), Monk (15)

SECOND ATTACK

Training in second attack allows the character a chance at additional strikes in combat — although a 100% second attack does not guarantee two attacks every round. Any class may learn this skill, although clerics and mages have a very hard time with it.

Learned by: All classes

THIRD ATTACK

Third attack gives a chance at an additional strike per combat round and also improves the chance of second attack. A perfect skill level does not guarantee three attacks every round.

Learned by: Warrior (12), Paladin (24), Assassin and Forgeborn (automatic at reclass)

BOOST
boostDuring battle only. Let out a battle cry to boost the next round of attacks.

By using a loud kiai, or battle cry, monks are able to attack more effectively for one round immediately following the boost. Using this ability repeatedly can wear the monk out, but it offers a large boost in hitroll and damroll for that brief moment, and remains a strong ability throughout the monk's career.

Learned by: Monk

CHAKRA
chakraManipulate your own chakra for a surge of health. Requires time between uses.

The monk's strength is not purely physical, but also extends to the spirit. Monks are attuned enough to manipulate their own chakra to gain a surge of health. The technique is advanced and requires time between each use, but can be used to recover from serious injuries. Monks cannot manipulate the chakras of others. This ability is similar to the paladin skill, lay hands.

Learned by: Monk

CHI EI KAZE
chi eiFocus chi inward — damage dealt to you is reflected back to the enemy.
chi kazeInfuse your muscles with chi — strike far more attacks than normal.

Also known as The Art of the Mirrored Soul. Chi is a special power imbued within everyone. Monks have long been trained to create and manipulate their chi. The Ei focuses chi inward so that any damage dealt to the monk is reflected back to the enemy as a blast of chi energy. The Kaze causes the monk to move extremely quickly, their chi infusing their body to deliver far more attacks than normal.

Learned by: Monk

STANCE TURTLE TIGER MANTIS SHADOW KENSAI
stance <name>Switch between fighting stances freely. Options: turtle, tiger, mantis, shadow (ninja), kensai (samurai).

Stances are open-handed fighting styles that monks can switch between freely as the situation demands, making them extremely flexible. All monks learn three basic stances; two advanced stances are gained by reclasses.

Turtle: Emphasizes body hardening and endurance. Fists become as tough as iron, delivering crushing attacks that can occasionally stun. Also increases maximum HP significantly.

Tiger: Emphasizes speed and aggression. Gains one additional attack per round; hitroll and damroll increase. Defenses (dodge, parry, shield block) are severely reduced.

Mantis: Emphasizes blocking and movement. Armor class increases with a high chance to guard against weapon attacks. Damage is severely reduced.

Shadow (Ninja reclass): The ninja becomes completely undetectable while fighting, phasing in and out and attacking from nowhere. While in shadow, the ninja is especially vulnerable to magic.

Kensai (Samurai reclass): Emphasizes the katana. Increases hitroll and allows the samurai to penetrate most defenses. The extreme concentration required prevents all chance of fleeing.

Learned by: Monk

SPIRIT EAGLE DRAGON DOLPHIN
dragonEmpower fists with flames.
dolphinEmpower fists with water.
eagleEmpower fists with electricity.

By calling upon the spirits of various animals, monks can imbue their fists and bodies with an element. All hand-to-hand attacks will be flagged with the appropriate element, and attackers may also suffer elemental effects. Monks can change spirits freely, but can only use one at a time.

Learned by: Monk

EVASION
evadeSpend movement points to sidestep incoming weapon attacks for one round. Does not block spells or other skills.

Ninjas have developed reflexes far beyond those of other characters. By concentrating intently on incoming attacks for a round, a ninja is able to easily sidestep any weapon attacks that would have connected.

Learned by: Monk (28), Ninja (automatic at reclass)

NINJUTSU
ninjutsu <tool> [target]Use a charm to inflict damage and apply a random debuff to the target. No mana required — costs a charm.

Ninjas have developed a series of tools, called charms, that they can use to great effect. Although it is not magic and requires no mana, ninjutsu inflicts a variety of damage based on the type of charm used. In addition, ninjutsu hinders the opponent with random effects — sometimes losing strength, attack speed, or spell damage. Ninjutsu is very fast as it requires no casting time, but is not very reliable and fails often.

Learned by: Monk (16), Ninja (automatic at reclass)

THIRD EYE
thirdTransfer any blindness affecting you to your current opponent.

This mystical skill allows the samurai to overcome blindness using their spiritual skills. A brilliant third eye flashes in the centre of the samurai's forehead, allowing them to see again while their foe is blinded in turn. Works against all types of blindness.

Learned by: Monk (24), Samurai (automatic at reclass)

QUICKEN
quickenHaste yourself and slow your current opponent simultaneously.

This mystical skill allows the samurai to fight with a sudden burst of speed. The samurai accelerates so rapidly that those they are fighting appear to move in slow motion.

Learned by: Monk (18), Samurai (automatic at reclass)

SEPPUKU
cast 'seppuku'Begin the ritual of self-sacrifice — grants a powerful surge of hit/dam for a short time before dealing HP damage to yourself.

This is a samurai's last chance at success. Once the ritual of Seppuku has started, the samurai becomes filled with a powerful surge of strength for a few moments before harming their own life. The standard method involves self-disembowelment followed by beheading, and is always harmful.

Learned by: Samurai (reclass only)

CRITICAL HITS

Rogues have lost a lot of their brute strength and as such are unable to make two backstabs while hasted. Their added nimbleness has enabled them to get critical hits on their victims with any attack they use. The extra damage dealt is proportional to the level of the thief. This skill is automatic during all attacks.

Learned by: Rogues

Passive Skills
FAST HEALING MEDITATION MEDITATE

Passive skills improve your character automatically without needing to be activated. The two most universal are fast healing and meditation, which accelerate HP and mana regeneration respectively.

Fast HealingSpeeds up hit point regeneration. After years of combat and recovery, many beings have developed the ability to cut the required rejuvenation period nearly in half.
MeditationSpeeds up mana regeneration. The ancient arts of meditation, when mastered, allow spiritual energies to be more vigorously renewed.

Fast Healing — All classes (Warrior 6, Cleric/Druid 9, Paladin/Ranger 10, Mage/Conjurer/Enchanter/Invoker 15, Runist/Monk 16)

Meditation — All classes (Mage/Conjurer/Enchanter/Invoker/Cleric/Druid 6, Paladin 10, Warrior/Ranger/Thief 15, Runist/Monk 16)

See also: Combat › Recovery for resting and sleeping mechanics.

REGENERATION

Regeneration is the magical healing power of the troll. It acts like the fast healing skill but heals more damage per tick, and also restores a small amount of health every combat round.

Learned by: Troll

TRANSFERANCE
transferanceTemporarily switches your durability and spellcasting reserves.

Learned by: Cleric (22), Druid (22)

DAGGER

Dagger is a weapon skill covering the use of all dagger-type weapons. All classes begin with basic proficiency, but improvement depends on practice. Several classes receive it automatically through their group.

Learned by: All classes (level 1)

DODGE

The best way to block a blow is to not be where it lands. The dodge skill improves the character's natural agility to the point where many blows will miss entirely. The chance of dodging is also affected by the dexterity of the attacker and the target.

Learned by: All classes

ENHANCED DAMAGE

With enough combat experience, a character can learn to strike with greater force. Enhanced damage is checked on each hit; at low skill levels the bonus triggers rarely, but it becomes reliable with practice.

Learned by: Warrior (1), Paladin (1), Ranger (1), Monk (1), Thief (18), Runist (10), Cleric (30), Druid (30), Mage (35), Conjurer (35), Enchanter (35), Invoker (35)

HAND TO HAND

Hand to hand combat gives a character a weapon even when disarmed — bare hands. Trained practitioners are far more effective than many swordsmen. All classes except Mage, Conjurer, Enchanter, and Invoker can learn this at reasonable levels; caster classes have higher minimum levels.

Learned by: All classes

PARRY

If at first you fail to dodge, block it. Parry is useful for deflecting attacks, and is successful more often than dodge. Parry requires a weapon for full success; the hand-to-hand skill may also be used, but results in reduced damage instead of no damage. The best chance of parrying occurs when the defender is skilled in both his and his opponent's weapon type.

Learned by: All classes

SHOULDER THROW

Automatic skill. Monks are nimble enough to use momentum against their foes. Anyone who attempts to bash a monk could find themselves flat on the ground as the monk catches them and slams them to the floor. This ability damages the opponent and stuns them instead of the monk.

Learned by: Monk

WEAPON CATCH

Automatic ability. Fighting with both hands free, monks are able to perform an expert disarm in which they have a chance to catch the weapon of anyone they successfully disarm, ending with the weapon in their inventory. Requires the disarm skill.

Learned by: Monk

INTENSE DAMAGE

The raw physical strength developed from using the body itself as a weapon allows monks to deal additional damage with each attack. Intense damage increases the base damage done by all attacks, including hand to hand and weapons. It stacks with enhanced damage and extreme damage.

Learned by: Monk (25)

INSTANT STAND

Automatic skill. Allows a monk to instantly recover from being knocked down, bashed, or tripped without losing a combat round.

Learned by: Monk (18), Ninja (automatic at reclass)

BLINDFIGHTING

Automatic ability. After much practice, ninjas are able to fight without penalty while blinded. Using their other senses, they can fight normally without the use of their eyes. Ninjas cannot actually see while blinded, but none of their skills are penalized.

Learned by: Ninja (reclass only)

BALANCE

Automatic ability. Using a quick acrobatic flip, ninjas are able to spring to their feet and resume fighting without penalty at the start of any round where they were knocked down and stunned. This allows a ninja to recover more quickly from the devastating stun inflicted by bash, trip, lightning, or other stunning effects.

Learned by: Ninja (reclass only)

CORPSE DRAIN

Automatic passive skill. Triggers when a character sacrifices a corpse. On success, the character absorbs residual life energy from the corpse, restoring a small amount of HP based on the corpse's level.

Learned by: All classes (2)

KATANA

The weapon skill required to wield katana-class weapons effectively. May be specialized for additional proficiency.

Learned by: Samurai (reclass only)

DOUBLESTRIKE

Automatic skill. Samurai are fierce and accurate with higher level katanas, and are able to deal damage twice with a single hit. This is not the same as second attack — if the first strike hits, the second will also hit and deal the exact same damage. Samurai may only doublestrike while wielding a katana.

Learned by: Samurai (reclass only)

EXTREME DAMAGE

Automatic skill. Samurai alone can strike with the accuracy required by this skill. Extreme damage raises the base damage done by all attacks from the samurai, including hand to hand and weapons. It stacks with enhanced damage and intense damage.

Learned by: Monk (40), Samurai (automatic at reclass)

BLINDSIDE

Automatic passive skill. Activates when a thief attacks a target who is fighting someone else. Blindsiding increases hit chance and adds bonus damage (level/3 to both min and max damage).

Learned by: Thief (10)

ZANSHIN

Monks, through vigorous training in the ways of combat, may find that they can rejuvenate themselves even while under heavy stress. Using the ancient technique of Zanshin, monks regenerate at a rapid rate while in combat.

Learned by: Monk (42)

Aggressive Skills
STEAL
steal <object> <character>Attempts to take an item from another character's inventory.
steal coins <character>Attempts to steal coins from another character.

STEAL is a Thief-only skill. If you are caught stealing from another player, you will receive a THIEF flag, making you free for others to kill without penalty. You must practice the steal skill to improve your success rate.

See also: help thief

BACKSTAB BACK STAB
backstab <target>Strikes vital areas for significantly increased damage. The victim must not already be fighting.
bs <target>Shorthand for backstab.

Thieves are weak in toe-to-toe combat but are masters of the knife in the back. When hasted, a Thief will deal two backstabs as long as they are wielding a weapon. Rogues (thief reclass) focus more on dexterity and lose the double backstab, but gain the ability to deal crippling strikes and critical hits.

Learned by: Thief

PAIN TOUCH
pain <target>Strike vital pressure points to cause intense pain, reducing the victim's skill performance until it subsides. Does not affect spellcasting.

With a simple jab or slap, monks are able to induce tremendous pain in their victims by striking vital pressure points or dislocating joints. This ability causes no physical injury in itself, but causes the victim to perform all skills more poorly than normal until the pain subsides.

Learned by: Monk (35)

CHOKE HOLD CHOKE
choke <target>Delivers 2–4 choking strikes dealing drowning damage and applying a hitroll penalty per hit. Triggers the martial arts cooldown.

Delivers 2–4 choking strikes that deal drowning damage and apply a hitroll penalty to the victim for each hit that lands. Triggers the martial arts cooldown on the monk, preventing another martial arts move immediately after.

Learned by: Monk (28)

BARRAGE
barrageFire a rapid volley of up to 6 ranged shots in a single round.

Rangers can fire a rapid volley of up to 6 ranged shots in a single round. Each shot is less accurate than a single aimed shot, but the combined damage can be devastating. Requires marksmanship.

Learned by: Ranger

BASH
bash <target>A powerful charge that knocks the target to the ground, leaving them stunned briefly.

Effectiveness depends on size difference, strength, and the target's dexterity. Elves are significantly harder to bash.

Learned by: Warrior (1), all other classes (8). Giants receive this skill at creation.

BATTLEROAR
battleroarSlam claws into the earth and unleash a thunderous roar — buffs allies and damages all enemies in the room.

Bronze Copper Dragons can slam their claws into the earth and unleash a thunderous roar. On success, the dragon and all group members gain hitroll and damroll bonuses for level/10 ticks. All enemies in the room take earth damage and receive a DEX penalty. The roar echoes throughout the entire area. Costs 40 movement.

Learned by: Bronze Copper Dragon (remort only)

DRAGON KICK
dragon <target>Strike in a flurry of kicks so fast it burns the air around them. Can also stun the victim.

The art of the dragon kick is legendary, going back millennia. Monks studied fire breathing red dragons, contemplating how the initial blast of flames was more damaging than the heat itself. They worked to imitate that force as a weapon of the body, developing the fearsome dragon kick — a flurry of kicks so fast it burns the very air around them.

Learned by: Monk

EAGLE CLAW
eagle <victim>Strike rapidly with the hands, hard enough to blast armor aside and allow subsequent attacks to hit more vulnerable areas.

For many centuries different monk groups have studied how to penetrate the tougher defense of larger and more armored foes. Thus was created the eagle claw attack — able to strike with the hands rapidly against a foe, with enough force to blast armor aside and allow subsequent attacks to hit more vulnerable areas.

Learned by: Monk (automatic)

DEMON FIST
demon <victim>Infuse a single punch with negative energy, blasting it into the victim and causing their deity to ignore them briefly when they attempt to recall.

Far beyond basic maneuvers, the demon fist calls upon the monk's chi, infusing a single attack with negative energy. This functions similarly to a curse spell — the victim's deity ignores them for a short while when they attempt to recall out of a situation.

Learned by: Monk (32)

SMOKEBOMB
smokebombFling a ball of smoke, ash, and dust at your feet to temporarily blind nearby foes.

This ability enables a thief to fling a special ball filled with smoke, ash, and dust at their feet, blinding foes temporarily — similar to kicking dirt at them.

Learned by: Thief (18)

BLEEDER ARROW
bleeder <victim>Graze an opponent with a ranged attack, causing massive bleeding with little actual damage. Victims leave a blood trail.

Rangers can intentionally graze their opponents with a ranged attack, causing massive bleeding but little actual damage. The victim will bleed out health slowly, and if they move around they will leave a blood trail that can lead the ranger to their prey. This action can be performed faster out of combat, but at a higher cost.

Learned by: Ranger

PINDOWN
pindown <target>Hit a critical joint to slow the target in combat, or pin a knee or foot out of combat to cause them to frequently collapse when running.

Rangers are excellent marksmen. Taking careful aim, they can hit a critical joint on their opponent. In standard combat this slows them down. Out of combat, the ranger always shoots for a knee or foot, causing the enemy to frequently collapse when running. This command can be performed faster out of combat, but at a higher cost.

Learned by: Ranger

HARASS
harassTurn the tides of battle by making everyone in the room not grouped with you focus their fighting on you.

This skill allows the zealot to turn the tides of the battle by making everyone in the room that is not grouped focus their fighting on the zealot.

Learned by: Zealot (reclass only)

Remort Skills
TASTE
taste <food|drink>Tastes a small amount of food or drink to determine whether it is poisoned.

Learned by: All remorts

DARK VISION DARKVISION

Allows remort dwarves and other races to see clearly in the dark, eliminating the need for a light source.

STONE SLEEP

Gargoyle remorts can channel healing energies while asleep, regenerating damage and energy faster than normal sleep. Warning: stone sleep is not easily controlled — it can overcome the gargoyle's better judgement and cause them to fall asleep at inopportune times.

Learned by: Sentinel Gargoyle, Haunting Gargoyle (remort only)

GORE

Sentinel gargoyles have grown long horns that serve well in combat, allowing them to thrust into the belly of whatever creature they are fighting.

Learned by: Sentinel Gargoyle (remort only)

CARVE BOULDER
carve boulderCarves a boulder in forests, fields, hills, mountains, or deserts, then throws it at an opponent for damage.

Learned by: All giant remorts

WATERWALK

Allows the character to walk on water, eliminating the need for a boat or fly spell.

Learned by: Doppleganger, Medusa (remort only)

BEHEAD BEHEADING

A passive combat skill that triggers automatically in melee. The character lunges at the opponent's neck — on a successful check, the attack deals increased damage with a chance of an especially vicious throat slash. Requires a wielded weapon.

Learned by: All classes at level 34

THRUST

Ruby dragons have a special skill. Should their parry be successful, they receive the chance to thrust their weapon at their foe, earning them an extra attack.

Learned by: Ruby Dragon (remort only)

DRAGON BITE BITE

Fire dragons have the ability to bite their victim should their dodge be successful, earning them an extra attack.

Learned by: Fire Dragon (remort only)

TAIL TAIL ATTACK

All remort dragons have gained the ability to use their long tails in combat. During a fight, the dragon can automatically lash out with their tail like a whip, dealing a fair amount of damage on contact.

Learned by: All dragon remorts (automatic at reclass)

GEMOLOGY
gemology <gem>Converts a precious stone into a projectile and hurls it at an opponent.

Learned by: Sapphire Dragon (remort only)

DRAIN CORPSE

Stinging slivers can supplement their diet by forcibly draining all nutrients from a corpse of something they have slain. This restores health to the sliver, but renders the corpse unsuitable for butchering. Drain Corpse triggers automatically when the corpse is sacrificed.

Learned by: Stinging Sliver (remort only)
