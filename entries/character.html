Equipment
CRAFT ITEM WORKSHOP
craft <keywords>Combines component items into a new crafted item. Must be in a workshop room.

The art of modifying and customizing equipment has resurfaced among the races of Terra and Dominia. With sufficient magical power, level, and the right items, you can combine them into new items.

All players receive the craft item skill automatically. Items which may form part of a crafted item will say so when lored or IDed. There will also be clues to locating the ingredients in the areas where the items are located.

You will need to locate a workshop-flagged room in order to craft. Crafting costs mana and consumes the component items. If you fail a craft attempt, you will not be able to attempt crafting again for quite some time.

Equipment can also be obtained through questing. See Questing for more information.

Learned by: All

OFFER OWNERSHIP CLAIM

All quested and crafted items are bonded with the one who first obtains them. This is visible to anyone who closely examines the item. If a bonded item's owner falls in combat, it will remain in their corpse (alongside potions, pills, etc.) rather than being lootable by others.

The offer command allows you to transfer full ownership of your items to someone else at a cost. The recipient must sacrifice gold (for crafted items) or quest points (for quest items) to complete the transfer. The fee is higher for transfers between clanners and non-clanners.

offer <item> <player>Lets the player know an item is being offered and shows the required sacrifice.
offer acceptIndicates willingness to make the sacrifice and accept the item.
offer refuseDeclines the offer.

Once the recipient accepts, the offering player repeats offer <item> <player> one more time to complete the transfer.

PROMISE WORSHIP MYENA

Myena has a promise she keeps to help the races of Dominia. By worshipping Myena, each race that starts on Dominia gets different abilities related to what that race does best. Trolls get extra regen, Gargoyles do extra damage, Slivers get double their remort bonus, and Kirre get bonus hand to hand abilities. All other races get double their regen rates.

HATRED WORSHIP KALLANA

Kallana has a strong hatred for races that are polar opposites of those she favors. By choosing to worship Kallana, you can channel this hatred for a short period of time. The following race pairings receive bonus damage when channeling Kallana's hatred: Sliver vs. Dragon, Dwarf vs. Elf, Human vs. Gargoyle, Troll vs. Giant, Kirre vs. Marid.

Wealth
GOLD SILVER MONEY

Midgaard's economy is based on gold and silver coinage (and the lowly copper, which you, as an adventurer, are far too proud to carry). Gold coins are worth 100 silver coins, but are also four times as heavy (25 to the pound, as opposed to 100 to the pound for silver). Gems can be bought to alleviate weight problems from the Midgaard's jeweler, who charges a mere 10% more than they are worth. 10 gems weigh one pound, and can be worth more than 100 gold pieces for the highest quality stones.

You can also deposit your money on a bank note. See help bank for more information.

AUCTION BID BET
auctionDisplays information on the item currently up for auction. Turns on auction channel if it is off.
auction <object>Places an item up for auction. Turns on auction channel if it is off.
auction bid <gold>Places a bid on the item up for auction.
auction bet <gold>Places a bid on the item up for auction.
auction stopCommand used by immortals to stop a suspicious use of the auction. (See also: help RULES)
noauctionToggles the auction channel on and off.

Auction is an automated mercantile system through which you can offer and sell items over the mud to the highest bidder. If no bidder makes an offer, the item will be returned to you and you can put it up for auction at a later time. Trash items cannot be auctioned. This is to prevent people from bidding large amounts of gold on an empty bank note, for example.

Remember that an immortal can confiscate your item at any time. If the auction channel is being abused, this can and may happen. Noauction will toggle the auction channel on/off for you. Typing auction while an auction is underway will display information on the object being offered and the latest bid received.

SPLIT
split <silver> <gold>Splits coins between you and all group members in the same room.

SPLIT splits some coins between you and all the members of your group who are in the same room as you. It's customary to SPLIT the loot after a kill. The first argument is the amount of silver to split (0 is acceptable), and the second is gold (optional).

Examples:
split 30 — split 30 silver
split 20 50 — split 20 silver, 50 gold
split 0 10 — split 10 gold

PASSWORD
password <old-password> <new-password>Changes your character's password. The first argument must be your current password; the second is the new one.

The PASSWORD command is protected against being snooped and against log files. Please write down your new password until you have become accustomed to typing it.

QUIT RENT SAVE
quitLeaves the game. Automatically saves your character first.
saveSaves your character and objects manually.

SAVE saves your character and objects. The game saves automatically every 15 minutes, which is the preferred method. Typing save will block all other commands for about 20 seconds, so use it sparingly.

QUIT leaves the game. You may QUIT anywhere — when you return, you will be back in the same room. QUIT automatically performs a SAVE, so you can safely leave with just one command.

Some objects such as keys and potions may not be saved. Quitting outside of a town or designated hall will cause you to lose experience. There is no RENT in this mud — just SAVE and QUIT whenever you want to leave.

NAMES

Redemption does not enforce role-playing, however a naming convention restricts new characters to names acceptable in the context of medieval fantasy. If your name does not fit this theme, you may be renamed or deleted at any time with or without warning by the immortals.

The following guidelines apply to naming:

  • Numerical references are not permitted.
  • Names of a sexual or profane nature are not permitted.
  • Nonsensical names are not permitted.
  • Names from literature or history may not be appropriate.
  • Names which are too similar to existing characters may not be appropriate.
  • Use of real-life names of people, places, or objects may not be appropriate.
  • Compound names may not be appropriate.

Common sense generally applies. If you are asked to rename, you must comply. Most renaming will be done before level 10, but there may be exceptions.

UNREMORT UNRECLASS

Due to changes over time or poor decisions, it is possible to forsake your remort or reclass so that you may choose another one. Requirements:

  • You must be level 51.
  • You must acquire a special crafted item (and collect its components).
  • You must travel to a special location in the abyssal seam — full details will be found there.
remort forsakeForsake your current remort. You will not be delevelled, and all remort-unique skills and spells will be removed.
reclass forsakeForsake your current reclass. You will not be delevelled, and all reclass-unique skills and spells will be removed.

Note: to choose another remort or reclass you must still meet all normal requirements such as quest points.
