The World

Two continents. One war. No neutral ground.

🐉
Terra
Birthplace of the Dragons

Terra burns. Scorched peaks, molten rivers, and skies perpetually choked with ash — this is the domain of dragonkind. Defeated in the First War, the Dragons retreated into shadow, nursing their wounds and their rage. They have not forgotten. They have not forgiven. And now, they are ready.

🌿
Dominia
Continent of the Slivers

Dominia is a land shaped by ancient power — dense forests, crumbling battlefields, and the ruins of a civilization that survived the First War. The Slivers, a hive-minded race of terrifying adaptive warriors, rose to dominance through relentless evolution. No two Slivers are alike; no army has ever broken their unity.

The First War ended with the Slivers standing victorious over a shattered Dragon empire. It was meant to be the last war — an age of uneasy peace settled across both continents. But peace is a fragile thing, and the Dragons are anything but broken.

Scouts report Dragon sightings in numbers not seen in a generation. Metallic dragons, once thought to be myth, have reemerged — reborn, remade, and more dangerous than ever. The elemental forces themselves seem to be shifting, as though the world is choosing a side.

In the middle of all of this stands you. Adventurer. Warrior. Mage. Whatever name the world gives you — the war does not care. It only asks: whose banner will you carry?

What Awaits You

A living world with systems that reward your choices.

Player vs Player Combat

Choose your path — engage in full player killing or enter structured player duels. A hardcore mode raises the stakes even higher, with real consequences for those who fall. Glory awaits the bold; death awaits the reckless.

Crafting

Forge your own legend — literally. Redemption's crafting system lets you create weapons, armor, and items that carry your mark. Master the forge, source rare materials, and build equipment no merchant could ever sell you.

Questing

From simple errands to world-altering sagas, Redemption's quest system spans the full breadth of both continents. Follow the threads of the war, uncover ancient secrets, and earn rewards that shape your character's path.

Class Change System

Change your class without losing your hard-earned progress. Redemption's class change system preserves what you've built — skills, experience, and growth — while letting you explore a new path when the mood strikes.

Remort & Reclass

Reach the pinnacle of power and then transcend it. The remort and reclass system lets endgame characters evolve into something greater — new abilities, new identities, and access to paths unavailable to those who haven't earned them.

Elemental Wheel

A fully realized system of elemental forces — fire, water, earth, air, shadow, and more — governs magic, combat, and the world itself. Elements interact, counter, and amplify each other. Master the wheel to master the battlefield.
